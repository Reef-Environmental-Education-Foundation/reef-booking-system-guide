Free Event Check-In — Staff Guide

Pick your event once, then check people in from that event's page.

Updated 2026-09-25 (new Event Check-In interface — one page per event)

Where to Start new 2026-09-25

Same starting point every time, no matter what the person tells you at the table.

1
Open Start Here - Pick Your Event

In Airtable, open the Event Check-In interface and go to Start Here - Pick Your Event: direct link. The Today tab shows today's events; All events shows the last 2 days and the next 2 weeks.

2
Tap your event

That event's page is your check-in station for the rest of the session — everything you do there is saved to that event only. The top of the page shows the event name, date/time, location and a live Check-in Count (date/time and location can't be edited here).

Two events at once? Each event has its own page, so two check-in tables never interfere. Tip: keep your event page open in its own browser tab — Steps 2 and 3 open in new tabs, and you'll come back to this one.

Step 1 — Check In Someone Already in Our System

Most people are found here. Always try this first.

1
Click the blue Add event button

It's under STEP 1 - Check in a participant | CHECKED IN (newest first). Airtable labels the button "Add event" — it adds a check-in to this event.

2
Type the person's name in the Attendee box

In the new row, click the Attendee box and type their name. Search matches names only, not email.

3
Pick them from the list

Their email fills in next to the name — use it to make sure you picked the right person when two people have similar names.

4
They appear at the top of the Checked In list

That's your confirmation — there's no pop-up. On to the next person.

Can't find them?

Don't create anyone here — go to Step 2.

Step 2 — Not Found? Check If They're a REEF Member

Use the NOT FOUND IN STEP 1? box at the bottom of the event page.

Search REEF Members new 2026-09-25

  1. Click the blue Step 2: Search REEF Members button. It opens the REEF member search in a new tab.
  2. Search their name or email and tap their name.
  3. Confirm it's them (email, city, member number), then click the green Add this member button at the top right.
  4. Read Result - what to do next on that page:
    If it says…Do this
    "Added to our system" or "Found their existing record and linked their membership"Go back to your event tab and check them in with Step 1.
    "Already in our system as [name]"Go back and check in that name with Step 1.
    "Not added: someone with this email is already in our system"Go back, search for the name on that existing record in Step 1, and check them in. It has been flagged for an admin to review — you don't need to do anything else.
Why the extra step?

Add this member checks for an existing person first, so we don't end up with two records for the same person. If it finds one, it links their membership to it instead of creating a duplicate.

Step 3 — Brand-New Participant

Only use Step 3 after Steps 1 and 2 come up empty.

Add New Participant checks in automatically

  1. In the same NOT FOUND IN STEP 1? box, click Step 3: Add New Participant. A short form opens in a new tab.
  2. Fill in Full name (required), Email (please ask — it lets us match them to their REEF membership), Phone, ZIP code, and Which event are they checking in to? (required — only today's events are listed).
  3. Click Add & Check In. They're checked in automatically and appear in your event's Checked In list within a few seconds. Close the form tab and go back to your event page.

When Something's Different

Each Checked In row shows Contact Info (last confirmed) (Confirmed or Needs Review) and a confirm checkbox. Click Open on a row to see the full check-in panel with the same boxes. None of these require leaving the event page.

Confirm their contact info (optional but helpful) new 2026-09-25

  1. If Contact Info says Needs Review, read their name, email and phone back to them.
  2. If it's correct, tick Read their contact info back - correct? Tick to confirm (in the row or in the panel). That automatically records today as their confirmation date — you no longer type a date on their profile.
  3. Confirmed means someone confirmed it within the last year, so you can skip asking.

Their email, phone, address or name spelling is wrong unchanged process

  1. Don't edit their record.
  2. Open their check-in row and tick Profile Update Requested at Check-in. A Pending Review request is created automatically and appears under Member Profile Update Requests on that check-in.
  3. Tap it and fill in Field Changed and New Value (Old Value if you know it). Never write corrections in the Notes field on their profile.
  4. Don't also tick the confirm box. Ticking the Profile Update box again later won't create a second request.

Two similar names and you're not sure you picked the right one

  1. Check in the closest match — don't leave anyone un-checked-in over this.
  2. Open the row and tick Not sure you picked the right person? Tick for review.
  3. Don't create a second person to be safe — an admin reviews these after the event.

You picked the wrong person

  1. Open the row and change the Attendee.
  2. Changing the Attendee on a row that's already checked in changes who that check-in belongs to, so do it carefully.

What Changed on 2026-09-25

New Event Check-In interface what changed 2026-09-25
  • New Event Check-In interface: pick the event once; each event has its own check-in page.
  • You check people in from the event page (not from each person's profile).
  • Member search now protects against duplicates: Add this member links an existing person instead of creating a second one, and flags unclear cases for admin review.
  • New participants are checked in automatically from the Step 3 form.
  • Contact info confirmation is now a checkbox on the check-in instead of typing a date on the profile.
  • The old Free Event Check-In pages (0, 1 and 2) are retired and marked "OLD - DO NOT USE" — don't use them. Admin queues (Membership Follow-Up, Profile Corrections, Check-ins by Event, Possible Duplicates, Members Linked to 2+ People) live in the Admin Event Check-In interface.
The short version: open Start Here - Pick Your Event, tap your event, and check people in from that page. Not found? Step 2 searches REEF members. Still not found? Step 3 adds them and checks them in. Wrong info or unsure of the match? Tick the box on their check-in row — an admin takes it from there.
