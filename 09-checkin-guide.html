Checking people in at REEF events

One guide for every event. Use the first tab for any single event. Use the second tab for REEF Fest, Oct 14-18, 2026, where each person checks in once for the whole festival.

Open the check-in tool
The one rule: search first, then check in. Never create a second record for someone just to be safe, and never link someone to a member record by name alone.

At the check-in table

Works on an iPad, laptop or phone. Each event has its own check-in page, and everything you do there is saved to that event only.

  1. Pick your event

    Open the tool. It starts on Start Here - Pick Your Event. The Today tab shows today's events. Use All events for anything else. Tap your event to open its check-in page.

  2. Add the person in Step 1

    Click the blue Add button and type their name. Pick them from the list. Their email shows next to each name to tell people apart. They appear at the top of the list, and that is your confirmation.

  3. Read their info back

    "I have you as Jane, jane@email.com. Still right?" If something is wrong, tell a team member after the event. Don't change their record at the table.

  4. Show or read the contact notice

    Every person sees or hears this once: "REEF may use your email to send information about this activity and occasional updates about marine conservation, programs, events, and ways to get involved. You can unsubscribe at any time. REEF does not sell your personal information." If they say they do not want updates, tick I do not want to receive REEF news and updates beyond communications related to this activity. on their row. Leave it unticked otherwise. Opting out only stops general REEF news. They still get emails about this activity.

  5. Ask how they heard about us

    Pick their answer in that column on their row. Add any detail under Anything more. The count at the top of the page shows who still needs an answer.

If you can't find them

Try these in order. Each one ends with the person checked in.

Step 2

Search REEF Members

Opens in a new tab. Find them, click Add this member, then come back and check them in with Step 1.

Step 3

Add New Participant

For someone brand new. Name, email and the event are enough. The contact notice and opt-out box are on the form. They are checked in automatically when you submit.

What staff do not do

You never sign anyone up for membership or unsubscribe them at the table. The team exports new attendees and their opt-out answers from this base afterward and handles membership separately.

The one rule for REEF Fest: every adult checks in once, on any day, at any session. That gives us the number of unique people who came, and an email for each so we can follow up. Nobody re-checks in for the next day or the next seminar. Give each person a name tag or wristband so you can tell who has already checked in.

Three ways people arrive

All three end the same way: one festival check-in per adult.

Registered online

Search, confirm, check in

Registrants from reef.org are already in the system. Search their name, read back their email, check them in. Each person in a party checks in individually.

Member or past attendee

Search, confirm, check in

Use Step 2, Search REEF Members. Confirm their info out loud, then check them in.

Brand new

Add, then check in

Use Step 3, Add New Participant. Name, email and how they heard about us are enough. The contact notice and the opt-out box are on that form, so the person can read and tick it themselves.

At the check-in table

  1. Search by name or email

    Type in the search bar and open their row. Never create a second record just to be safe.

  2. Read their info back

    "I have you as Jane, jane@email.com. Still right?" If a Confirm at Check-in prompt shows, ask that question (for example, whether a shared email is still theirs). Don't link anyone to a member by name alone.

  3. Show or read the contact notice

    Every person sees or hears the notice once, as on the Any event tab. If they say they do not want updates, tick the opt-out box on their row. Leave it unticked otherwise. They are checked in as normal either way.

  4. Ask how they heard about REEF Fest

    Ask once, at this check-in only. Registered guests get asked too, because the registration form did not include the question.

  5. Check them in

    Save the check-in. They are now counted for the whole festival.

  6. Count minors, once per party

    If children came with them, enter how many in Minors (under 18) in your party on one adult only. Children do not check in, and we never ask or record why someone isn't checking in.
