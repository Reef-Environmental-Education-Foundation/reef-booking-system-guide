The Four (Really Five) Systems

EPO Airtable

Owns: people, organizations, and the earliest stage of an inquiry — before it's worth pricing.

Covers: prospect → Inquiry only. Once a record is checked "Ready for REEF Bookings," EPO keeps a read-only mirror of Status via OXP Booking Status, but REEF Bookings is now the authority on deal stage.

Also holds: vendor and contact identity that Bookings and Vendors records link back to (EPO Organization Record ID / EPO Record ID fields) — don't re-key contact info directly in Bookings if it already exists in EPO.

REEF Bookings Airtable

Owns: pricing, proposals, and the booking lifecycle from the moment a record graduates out of EPO.

Covers: Quoting → Proposal Sent → Verbal Yes → Contracted → Confirmed → In Progress → Completed (+ Cancelled, any time). See the 8-Stage Bookings Status Guide for what each stage means.

Rule of thumb: once a booking exists in REEF Bookings, its Status field is the single authority on deal stage — not EPO, not Asana, not a spreadsheet.

Asana

Owns: staff tasks, reminders, rollout/testing work, and work coordination — the to-do list, not the deal-stage authority.

Also owns: the review-gate task. Whenever a proposal or contract package is generated for a booking, a review task is created here (assigned to Rose) — nothing goes to a customer until that's checked off. This is currently the only place that gate lives; it isn't mirrored anywhere else.

Scheduling

Owns: staffing needs and assignments, once a program is real enough to staff. Not yet wired to auto-receive staffing demand from a Confirmed booking — today this is a manual heads-up from whoever's coordinating the booking to whoever assigns staff.

Google Drive / the Proposal Generator Sheet

Owns: System 1's generated proposals (Google Doc/PDF) and supporting files — this is what every real customer sees today. The "OXP Proposal Generator" Sheet pulls straight from REEF Bookings' numbers; it doesn't write anything back.

Quick Reference — Where to Look, by Question

QuestionCheck this
Is this a real lead worth pricing?EPO — Opportunity Stage
What's this booking's actual deal stage?REEF Bookings — Status field, always (never EPO's mirror, never a spreadsheet)
What's the price / payment schedule?REEF Bookings — the formula fields on the record
Has someone reviewed this proposal/contract before it went out?Asana — look for a review task assigned to Rose linked to this booking
What does staff still need to do on this booking?Asana task, and the Bookings record's Next Action / Follow-up Date fields
Who's staffing this program?Scheduling (manual handoff today — ask the Coordinator if unsure)
What did the customer actually see?Google Drive proposal Sheet/PDF (System 1) — not the HTML System 2 page unless you know this booking was a staff test
A booking's facts can live in up to three places at once — Asana, a Dropbox tracking spreadsheet some Coordinators still keep, and the REEF Bookings Airtable record — with nothing cross-referencing them automatically. When two disagree, trust REEF Bookings' own fields first, then update the others by hand to match. Don't assume they're already in sync.
